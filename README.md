__Features:__
- 6DOF Controller-aimed Gun combat with 1:1 iron sights
- 6DOF Gesture-based Melee - swing Left Controller for a Quick Attack, hold LT while swinging for Heavy Attack and gun combat
- Occulobe Enhancement System (R3) switches weapon aim from Right Controller to HMD-based aiming
- Hold Right Thumbrest to display the UI, release to hide
- Hold both Thumbrests to activate the Psygate
- Gesture DPad – Hold Right Thumbrest and move controller Up,Down, Left, or Right to press the corresponding D-Pad direction (Functionality is also duplicated while holding RB)
- Tunable haptics for in-game actions and events (disable in-game Controller Rumble)
- Right Stick tuning – smoother turning acceleration ramp for improved player rotation. Ensure in-game Controller sensitivity is set to the default value of 30%


__Installation:__
- Use this joeyhodge backend or later: https://github.com/joeyhodge/UEVR/releases/tag/081326DaysGoneNOMO
- Inject any time


__Controls:__

Quest Controller mappings:

  -Left Controller-
  Left Stick - Move Player, navigate menus
  Left Grip - Block
  Left Trigger - Hold to charge Power Attack
  X Button - Ability 1, Cancel
  Y Button - Ability 2
  B + Y Button - Ability 3
  L3 - Click once to sprint, Hold for Start
  Menu - Displays Pause Menu

  -Right Controller-
  Right Stick - Rotate Player Left/Right
  Right Grip (hold) - Comms Menu - use Right Stick to choose the Order to issue. When ordering Brother Nahum to Heal, gesture Down/Up with the Right Controller to choose to heal either he or Brother Barachiel.
  Right Trigger - Fire Weapon
  B Button - Reload
  A Button - Interact/Confirm
  R3 - Click once to activate the Occulobe Enhancement System, switching aim to HMD. Hold R3 to Reset Helmet Position. Click to toggle Main Menu visibility.
  Right Thumbrest - Hold to view HUD, gesture Up/Down/Left/Right while holding for D-Pad, Hold Both Thumbrests to activate Psygate.
  

__Notes: __
- Occulobe Enhancement System (R3) currently has no zoom or visual enemy identification aid
- Non-Steam versions are unsupported
- Map Screen does not display map geometry – may not be fixable
- Right Arm and Pauldron meshes have been hidden- they would clip into the viewport, and a space marine wouldn’t be able to see their own pauldrons or most of their arms anyway.
- The in-game crosshair is unreliable, and I have had no luck getting a satisfactory version working. Virtually all of the weapons in the game are hipfired anyway, gun mesh had been aligned with shot vector for reliable iron sights, and the Occulobe Enhancement System should do a good job of covering cases where it might have been needed.
