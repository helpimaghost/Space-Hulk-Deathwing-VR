**6DOF Profile V1.02 by help im a ghost**

Based on the above profile posted by Pande4360, Thanks to Pande4360, JoeyHodge, and all others who have contributed.

__Features:__
- 6DOF melee and gun combat
- Tunable haptics for in-game events
- UI hidden by default, hold Right Thumbrest (Quest) to display
- Right Stick tuning – smoother turning acceleration ramp for improved player rotation
- Gesture DPad (Quest) – Hold Right Thumbrest and move controller Up,Down, Left, or Right to press the corresponding D-Pad direction (Functionality is also duplicated while holding RB)
- Melee changes – swing Left Controller for a Quick Attack, hold LT while swinging for Heavy Attack
- Visor mode switches from Right Controller to HMD aiming

__Installation:__
- Use latest JoeyHodge Backend dll https://github.com/joeyhodge/UEVR/releases, specifically "071226LEGACYAIMFIX"+
- Inject any time
- Ensure in-game Controller sensitivity is set to the default value of 30%, and Controller Rumble is Disabled

__Controls:__
- Game Controller Defaults +
- R3 Hold: Reset Helmet Position
- L3 Hold: Start Button, e.g. Menu or CloseMenu
- Melee: swing Left Controller for a Quick Attack, hold LT while swinging for Heavy Attack

__Notes: __
- You may need to click The Right Stick twice after initial loading in to fix UI elements (WIP)
- Tabbing out of game after injecting will mess up Crosshair, or crosshair may not be visible.
- Special Visor currently has no zoom or visual enemy identification aid
- Non-Steam versions are unsupported
- Map Screen does not display map geometry – may not be fixable
- Right Arm and Pauldron meshes have been hidden- they would clip into the viewport, and a space marine wouldn’t be able to see their own pauldrons or most of their arms anyway.
- I never did see a reliable crosshair and ultimately turned all of that off, as it feels unnecessary anyway and was visually distracting. Virtually all of the weapons in the game are hipfired anyway. Visor Scope should do a good job of covering cases where it might have been needed.
