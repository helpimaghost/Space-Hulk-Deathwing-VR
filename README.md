## Features

### Gunplay
- **6DOF Gun Combat** with 1:1 iron sights, active reloads, and haptics
- **Occulobe Enhancement System (R3)** HMD-based aim switch for precision shots
- **Lighting Enhancements**: plasma reactors, glass tubes and bolter lamps all cast light

### Active Reloading
- Press Reload and the magazine drops out of the gun. A ghost shows where the new one goes.
- Grip at your left hip to take a fresh magazine, insert it, and rack the slide if the chamber is empty.
- All Bolter weapons, as well as the Assault Cannon, Vengeance, and Heavy Flamer.

### Melee
- **Physical Melee**: Swing, stab, bash, smash and crush your enemies, either barehanded or with the powerful Psyker weapons at your disposal.
- **Force Sword and Axe**: Slash, stab, and stun enemies with a pommel bash or axe thrust. Invert your grip with a swipe of the left Thumbrest.
- **Lightning Claws & Thunder Hammer**: hits land on contact. Hold the trigger to charge the weapons with lightning, tearing them apart or sending them flying.
- **Storm Shield** Shield bash, parry, and block damage based on location.
- Left-handed weapons can be stowed over the left shoulder.

### Hand-to-Hand
- **Grab**: Hold Left Grip to grab and hold smaller enemies, carrying them around.
- **Slam** it into a wall or **squeeze the trigger** to crush your enemies.
- **Throw** it, alive or dead.
- **Punch**: a closed fist (Left Trigger) sends stealers flying.
- **Holster the Storm Bolter** at your hip (right grip, controller pointed at the floor) to free your right hand. Both hands can grab, punch and crush.

### Body & Tracking
- **Full arms**: forearms and upper arms follow your hands. The chest and pauldrons follow your body, not your head.
- **HMD-tracked torch** that shines where you look- reach up and press (Right Grip) to toggle it.

### Movement
- **L3 thruster sprint**.
- **Right stick tuning**: smoother turning acceleration. Keep the in-game controller sensitivity at the default **30%**.

### Interface
- **Peek HUD**: hold the right thumbrest to show the HUD. Important alerts appear on their own: low armour, a squadmate in the red, relics found, etc)
- **Psygate**: hold both thumbrests.
- **Gesture D-Pad**: hold the right thumbrest and move the controller up, down, left or right to press that D-Pad direction. Holding the right grip does the same thing.
- **VR menus**: Hold R3 in the main menu to hide the menu UI and look around a bit.

### Haptics
- Tunable haptics for recoil, melee, abilities, landing, footsteps, sprinting and reloading. 

### Visuals & Performance
- A custom UEVR backend delivers improved game-specific performance
- DLSS/DLAA implementation for improved performance
- Enhanced 4k Texture Pack, built for VR

## Controls (Quest)

### Left Controller

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Left Stick | Move player, navigate menus |
| Grip | Grab, Block |
| Trigger | **Hold:** Activate Weapon Power<br>**Double Tap:** Toggle |
| X | Ability 1 / Cancel |
| Y | Ability 2 |
| X + Y | Ability 3 |
| L3 | Click to sprint, hold for Start |
| Menu | Pause menu |
| Thumbrest | **Swipe:** Change Grip (Force Sword / Force Axe)<br>**Hold both thumbrests:** Psygate |

### Right Controller

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Right Stick | Rotate player left/right |
| Grip (hold) | Comms menu: use the right stick to choose an order. When ordering Brother Nahum to heal, gesture down or up with the right controller to heal either him or Brother Barachiel. |
| Trigger | Fire weapon |
| A | Interact / Confirm |
| B | Reload |
| R3 | **Click:** activate the Occulobe Enhancement System (aim switches to HMD)<br>**Hold:** reset helmet position<br>**Click:** toggle Main Menu visibility |
| Thumbrest | **Hold + gesture up/down/left/right:** D-Pad<br>**Hold both thumbrests:** Psygate |

## Notes

- The Occulobe Enhancement System (R3) does not zoom.
- Only the Steam version is supported.
- The Map Screen does not currently show map geometry.
- No in-game controller crosshair- Iron sights are tuned and accurate for all guns, and Occulobe provides an HMD-based reticle mode for precise shots.
